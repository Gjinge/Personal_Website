Mission Demolition.

Overview

A Unity coursework prototype exploring mouse input, rigid-body projectiles, castle structures, and camera tracking. The source separates launching, goal detection, level management, and projectile-line drawing into small components.

Implementation highlights

  • Slingshot input. Slingshot.cs constrains the drag distance and converts the release offset into rigid-body velocity.
  • Physics and camera. The scene includes a ground plane, slingshot, and castle structure. FollowCam.cs interpolates the camera toward a point of interest.
  • Level and goal logic. MissionDemolition.cs tracks shots and level state; Goal.cs detects goal contact. ProjectileLine.cs implements sampled path drawing.

Prototype notes

Work in progress: the saved scene has unassigned UI references and an empty level list; a launch check reported errors. The camera-switch cases also contain leading spaces, and the slingshot clears its projectile reference before assigning it to the line component. This page documents the prototype; it does not claim a fully validated game.

The download includes Assets, Packages, and ProjectSettings, including the original images, audio, materials, and code. Open it with the Unity version above; registry packages are restored on import. Known prototype issues remain, and clean-import gameplay validation has not been completed.
