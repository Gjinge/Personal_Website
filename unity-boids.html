Overview

The available project contains a Boid prefab and a scene with Attractor and BoidAnchor objects. It is included here as an early setup study, with its actual scene files available for inspection.

Implementation highlights

  • Scene structure. The scene includes named objects for an attractor and a boid anchor, alongside the camera, light, and rendering volume.
  • Reusable prefab. A Boid prefab is preserved in the selected source snapshot.
  • Current scope. No custom flocking C# scripts were found in the supplied project Assets. Separation, alignment, and cohesion are not claimed as implemented.
